M8MBC-586 clocks:

The M8MBC-586 main clock is generated by the RP2040 system controller. The main clock is 66 MHz and is used to drive the CPU, CTRL/DATA FPGAs, four CK inputs of the SDRAM.

The RP2040 generates the main clock using its internal PLL and outputs it via GPIO21 to CDCVF2310PW 1:10 fanout clock buffer (U605). The clock buffer distributes the main clock to the CPU, CTRL/DATA FPGAs, and four CK inputs of the SDRAM. The clock buffer is powered by 3.3V. All outputs are impedance matched by resistors at the beginning of each output.

As for the PCI main clock. It's generated by the CTRL FPGA. The CTRL FPGA generates 33 MHz (by dividing the 66 MHz main clock by 2, this keeps PCI in the same clock domain) and outputs it to the PCI slots and ISA FPGA. The PCI clock is also buffered by other clock buffer - the CDCV304 - 1:4 fanout clock buffer (U606). The PCI clock buffer is powered by 3.3V and all outputs are impedance matched by resistors at the beginning of each output.
