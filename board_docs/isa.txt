M8MBC-586 ISA bus:

The M8MBC-586 features an ISA bus implemented in the SB (ISA) FPGA. The SB FPGA acts as a PCI-ISA bridge, providing legacy ISA bus functionality. There are two 16-bit ISA slots on the board. 

The slots are fully wired to the SB FPGA, which handles all the necessary address decoding, data transfer, and control signal generation. The ISA bus operates at /4 of the PCI bus clock, which is typically 33.3 MHz, resulting in an ISA bus clock of 8.33 MHz.

The only omitted feature is MASTER signal as it complicates the design and is not needed for 90% of ISA cards. It's usually only used for obscure SCSI ISA cards.
